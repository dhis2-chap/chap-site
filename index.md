### A robust open-source platform for climate and health modeling

Chap is a Climate & Health Modeling Platform that brings together climate health models into a unified ecosystem, connecting researchers and cutting-edge epidimiological models to policy makers and health practitioners. It makes complex modeling workflows accessible, streamlines rigorous model evaluation, and integrates directly with DHIS2, the world's leading health information system.

[Learn more](https://chap.dhis2.org/about/index.md)

### Chap builds on and integrates with DHIS2 to support real-world use

**Chap Modeling Platform**

Python-based processing engine for model orchestration, data pipelines, validation, metrics, and hyperparameter tuning. It integrates seamlessly with DHIS2 system for accessing health data and disseminating forecasts, while also functioning as a standalone Python package and command-line tool.

[Get started](https://chap.dhis2.org/chap-modeling-platform/index.md)

**DHIS2 Modeling App**

Pilot app for DHIS2 that provides seamless integration with the Chap Modeling Platform, providing a user-friendly interface within DHIS2 for selecting data to use to generate predictions in Chap – including data from the DHIS2 Climate App and Climate Tools – train predictive models, visualize predictions, and evaluate model accuracy.

[Get started](https://chap.dhis2.org/chap-modeling-platform/modeling-app/index.md)

**DHIS2 Climate Data**

Chap Modeling Platform utilizes [DHIS2 Climate App](https://dhis2.org/climate/climate-data/) and [Climate Tool](https://climate-tools.dhis2.org/), which enable flexible incorporation of climate, weather, environmental, and other geospatial data into DHIS2. This data then flows through the DHIS2 Modeling App, where the user could select which data sources to be used by the Chap Modeling Platform for model evaluation and disease prediction.

[Get started](https://chap.dhis2.org/climate-data/index.md)
